## Overview

The platform handles commissions and payouts. It is currently **in its pilot phase**. It is not yet in full production use.

It has grown to roughly **75 modules**, which makes structure the central engineering problem. When there are this many parts, the system needs clear boundaries to stay understandable.

## My role

- I **led the architecture** of the platform.
- I **contributed to full-stack development** alongside two backend developers.
- I worked on the **database migrations** that moved the system's data model forward as it grew.

This was team work. The platform is the result of the three of us working together, not of one person.

## The engineering problem

Commission and payout systems sit where business rules meet money. Mistakes are expensive, and the rules change. That combination puts pressure on three things:

- **Module organisation.** Each module needs a clear responsibility, so the platform can keep growing without becoming tangled.
- **Data modelling.** The data model has to represent the business accurately and evolve safely.
- **Migrations.** Changes to the schema have to be repeatable and reviewable, and safe to run against real data.
