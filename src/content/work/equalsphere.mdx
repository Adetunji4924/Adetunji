## Overview

Equalsphere is a **workforce inclusion platform**. I co-founded it, and I serve as **Product Lead and Creative Director**. The platform is **in development** and has not launched yet.

## My role

My role sits between product and creative direction:

- **Product:** shaping what the platform does and how its modules fit together.
- **Creative direction:** making sure the experience and the brand express the same intent.

## How it is built

Equalsphere is built from **multiple product modules** on a **NestJS** backend. Working across both product and design means making sure design and engineering decisions support each other, rather than being handed from one to the other.
