## Overview

Vehtriq is my founder project: **AI-powered fleet intelligence** for operators who run vehicles as a business. It is **in development**. Everything on this page describes the product's direction and the architecture I have chosen. It is not a list of finished features.

## The problem

Small and medium-sized fleet operators often manage vehicles, drivers and costs across scattered tools, paperwork and messages. **Fuel cost** is a particularly important blind spot. It's one of the largest running costs and one of the hardest to see clearly.

The goal is simple to say: help operators **understand and manage what is happening across their fleet**.

## Product direction

The product is organised around the everyday work of running a fleet:

- **Vehicles**: the fleet itself
- **Drivers**: who is driving what
- **Maintenance**: keeping vehicles on the road
- **Trips**: what each vehicle is doing
- **Compliance**: documents and obligations
- **AI-assisted insights**: making sense of all of the above

## Architecture direction

I chose a **Cloudflare-first** architecture. Most of the system runs at the edge, with managed services where they fit.

- **API:** Hono on Cloudflare Workers.
- **Data:** Neon (PostgreSQL), connected through Hyperdrive.
- **Platform services:** R2, KV, Durable Objects and Queues, each used where it is the appropriate tool rather than by default.
- **AI:** a multi-model gateway. **Gemini** handles OCR-related workflows, for example reading documents. **Claude** handles narrative and diagnostic reasoning.

The multi-model choice is a deliberate trade-off. It gives each task the model best suited to it, at the cost of an extra layer to design and operate.

## Scope and trade-offs

The first audience is small and medium-sized operators, and fuel-cost visibility is the first problem. Narrowing the scope this way keeps the minimum viable product honest. It focuses on one problem that is clearly worth solving before expanding.
